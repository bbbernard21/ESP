Click on your name in the top-right corner, select "Profile", and click the "Edit Profile" button. You can update your personal information there.

Go to Settings > Security, enter your current password and your new password twice to confirm the change.

Click on "My Courses" in the navigation menu to see a list of all your enrolled courses.

Select a course from "My Courses" and click on "Course Materials" to access all available resources.

Go to Assessments > Assignments, select the assignment you want to submit, and click the "Submit" button to upload your work.

Common formats like PDF, DOC, DOCX, and ZIP are accepted. Check the assignment requirements for specific format guidelines.

Your GPA is calculated based on your course grades weighted by credit hours. A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0

Go to Progress > Academic Progress to view your overall progress, course grades, and performance analytics.

The portal works best with recent versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

Go to Support > Contact Support and select "Technical Support" from the category dropdown menu.
