Getting Started

Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is your central hub for managing your academic journey. Here you'll find:

  • Overall GPA and academic performance metrics
  • Enrolled courses and their current status
  • Upcoming assignments and deadlines
  • Recent activities and notifications

Navigation Menu

The main navigation menu provides quick access to:

  • Course materials and schedules
  • Assignments and assessments
  • Academic progress tracking
  • Communication tools
  • Support resources

Course Management

Viewing Courses

Access your courses through the "My Courses" dropdown menu:

  • View course details, including syllabus and schedule
  • Access course materials and resources
  • Track course progress and grades
  • Communicate with professors and classmates

Course Materials

Each course provides access to:

  • Lecture notes and presentations
  • Required readings and textbooks
  • Supplementary resources
  • Study guides and practice materials

Assessments

Managing Assignments

Keep track of your assignments through the Assessments menu:

  • View assignment details and requirements
  • Submit completed work
  • Track submission status and grades
  • Receive feedback from professors

Exams and Quizzes

Prepare for and complete assessments:

  • View exam schedules and requirements
  • Access practice quizzes and study materials
  • Submit online assessments
  • Review grades and feedback

Academic Progress

Progress Tracking

Monitor your academic progress through various tools:

  • Track overall GPA and course grades
  • Set and monitor academic goals
  • View performance analytics
  • Generate progress reports

Academic Goals

Set and manage your academic goals:

  • Create course-specific goals
  • Track goal progress
  • Receive progress notifications
  • Adjust goals as needed
